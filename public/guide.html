Get started in three steps

You will need your glasses, a phone or computer, and your WebUntis sign-in.

01

Show a pairing code

Open the glasses view on your Meta display. Keep it open while you use the six-digit code.

02

Connect WebUntis securely

Open the web app on another device. Enter the code, WebUntis server, school identifier, username, and your normal WebUntis password.

03

See your timetable

Your next lesson appears on the glasses after sign-in. The WebUntis session stays saved and renews automatically if it expires.

Good to know: Your credentials and WebUntis session are stored encrypted. The glasses receive only a device ID. You should not need to sign in again after pairing.

Frequently asked questions

Which password should I use?

Use your normal WebUntis password. The WebUntis Mobile QR key is not a password and is not supported as a sign-in method in this version.

The code expired.

Codes last ten minutes. Reload the glasses view for a new code, then enter it during setup.

How do I disconnect my account?

Choose “Disconnect” in the web app or on the glasses. This removes the encrypted profile and pairing from Cloudflare D1.
